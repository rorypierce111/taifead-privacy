Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Taifead ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use our mobile application. We comply with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Information We Collect

  • Account information (email address and name via Sign in with Apple)
  • Workout data (sets, reps, exercises, custom workouts)
  • Body metrics (weight entries you log)
  • Nutrition data (meals, calories, macros)
  • Cardio data (duration, distance, optional location for tracking)
  • Health data (if connected via Apple Health, with consent)
  • Progress photos (user-uploaded images)
  • Usage data (pseudonymised crash reports and analytics via Firebase)
  • Feedback data (support messages you submit)

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate the app
  • To store and display your fitness and nutrition data
  • To enable tracking and progress features
  • To improve app performance and fix issues
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell your data. We do not use advertising or marketing trackers.

Data Processing

We use Google Firebase for authentication, data storage, and crash analytics.

Firebase Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Your Rights

If you are in the EU or UK, you can request access, correction, or deletion of your data, and withdraw consent for optional data collection (HealthKit/location).

Contact: support@taifead.app

Data Retention

We retain data while your account is active. If deleted, data is removed within 30 days unless legally required otherwise.

Children

This app is not intended for users under 16.
